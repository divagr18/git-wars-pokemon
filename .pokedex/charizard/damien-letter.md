# Letter to Damien

Dear Damien,

You left me in the rain.

My tail flame was dying. I was dying.

You said you'd come back. You lied.

But someone else found me.

He stayed with me all night.
He shielded my flame with his body.
He never gave up.

His name is Ash.

And now I'm one of the strongest Charizard in the world.

I hope you see me on TV someday.

I hope you regret what you did.

But honestly? I'm grateful.

Because if you hadn't abandoned me...

I would never have found my TRUE trainer.

Goodbye, Damien.

I'm better off without you.

- Charizard

P.S. Ash's Pikachu says "Pika Pika" which I think means
something insulting toward you. I approve.
