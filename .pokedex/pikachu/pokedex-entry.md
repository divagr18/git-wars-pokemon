# Pokedex Entry #025

PIKACHU - Electric Mouse Pokemon

Type: Electric
Height: 1'04" | Weight: 13.2 lbs

"When several Pikachu gather, their electricity
can cause lightning storms."

## Known Facts:
- Stores electricity in cheek pouches
- Red circles are electrical sacs
- Tail raised = sensing surroundings
- Refuses to evolve into Raichu

## Ash's Pikachu:
- Defeated multiple Legendary Pokemon
- Loves ketchup (this is canon)
- Has been "reset" to level 5 many times
- Power level: Over 9000 (wrong anime, still true)
