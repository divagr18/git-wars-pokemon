# The Eeveelutions

Eevee's unstable genetic code allows unique evolution!

## Original Three:
- Vaporeon (Water) - Water Stone
- Jolteon (Electric) - Thunder Stone  
- Flareon (Fire) - Fire Stone

## Later Discoveries:
- Espeon (Psychic) - High friendship, daytime
- Umbreon (Dark) - High friendship, nighttime
- Leafeon (Grass) - Level up near Moss Rock
- Glaceon (Ice) - Level up near Ice Rock
- Sylveon (Fairy) - High affection + Fairy move

## Fun Facts:
- Vaporeon can dissolve into water
- Jolteon can't store electricity (oops)
- Flareon has no good Fire moves (meme)
- Umbreon glows in the dark
- Sylveon's ribbons sense emotions
