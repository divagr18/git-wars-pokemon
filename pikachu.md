## The Bond

At first, I wanted nothing to do with you.

You were late. Unprepared. Couldn't even catch me in a Poke Ball.

I shocked you. Multiple times. You deserved it.

But then... the Spearow came.

Dozens of angry birds, sharp beaks gleaming.

And you... you stood in front of me.

You took the hits meant for me. You shielded me with your own body.

"Pikachu... run away," you whispered. "Save yourself."

No.

No human had ever protected me before.

**PIKA... CHUUUUUU!!!**

The thunderbolt lit up the entire sky.

---

## Your Mission

We're partners now. But you came from somewhere else, didn't you?

Return to where you started this branch of the journey. Bring me with you.

When our worlds collide, there will be conflict. Let my electric will prevail!

After we're united, the rock-solid trainer Brock awaits in Pewter City.
