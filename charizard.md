## The Story of Charmander

Remember when you found me?

I was just a Charmander. Abandoned in the rain by my trainer Damien.

My tail flame was dying. I was dying.

But you... you stayed with me all night.

You shielded my flame with your own body.

You didn't give up on me.

---

## The Years of Disobedience

I evolved into Charmeleon. I became angry. I didn't listen.

I evolved into Charizard. I was proud. I ignored your commands.

You lost battles because of my arrogance.

Everyone told you to release me.

But you never did.

You never gave up on me.

Not once.
