## The Evolution Puzzle

I can become many things! Which evolution is correct?

**Water Stone** -> Vaporeon (Water) -> Digit would be: 3
**Thunder Stone** -> Jolteon (Electric) -> Digit would be: 5
**Fire Stone** -> Flareon (Fire) -> Digit would be: 7

Hmm... what should I become?

*Eevee looks at Pikachu*

*Pikachu sparks with electricity*

"Pika!"

Oh! Pikachu is Electric type! My best friend's element!

If I want to match my partner's energy... I should become...

**JOLTEON!**

---

## The Answer

The 7th digit is: **5**

(Thunder Stone = Jolteon = Electric = 5!)
