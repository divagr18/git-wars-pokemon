"I'm Ash Ketchum from Pallet Town, and I'm gonna be the greatest Pokemon Master!"

---

## About Ash

- Age: 10 (eternally)
- Hometown: Pallet Town
- Dream: Become a Pokemon Master
- Current Status: Overslept, missed starter Pokemon

---

## The Story

You woke up late. REALLY late.

By the time you reached Professor Oak's lab, Bulbasaur, Charmander, and Squirtle were all taken by other trainers.

But Oak had one more Pokemon...

"This one is... special," he said nervously. "It doesn't like being in a Poke Ball."

A yellow mouse Pokemon sat on the table, electricity sparking from its red cheeks.

"Pika..." it growled suspiciously.

You didn't care. You NEEDED a Pokemon!

---

## Your Mission

Pikachu is waiting for you on another branch. Find him!

Once you've bonded with Pikachu, bring him back here. But remember - Pikachu is stubborn. When there's a disagreement, let the electric mouse have his way!
