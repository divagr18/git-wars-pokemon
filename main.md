# Welcome to Professor Oak's Laboratory

*The door creaks open. You step into a room filled with books, research papers, and strange machines. An elderly man in a white lab coat turns to greet you.*

"Ah, there you are! I've been expecting you!"

"I am Professor Oak, the Pokemon Professor. Welcome to the world of Pokemon!"

"This world is inhabited by creatures called Pokemon. For some people, Pokemon are pets. Others use them for fights. As for myself... I study Pokemon as a profession."

---

## Your Journey Begins

You've come to Pallet Town with a dream: to become the Pokemon League Champion.

To achieve this, you must:
1. Catch Pokemon and build your team
2. Defeat 8 Gym Leaders to collect all badges
3. Challenge the Pokemon League

But first, you need a Pokemon partner!
